Privacy Policy

BomSaber — Last updated: August 2026

1. Data collection

BomSaber ("the App") does not collect, store, or process any personal data from end users. The App is a personal content-publishing tool used exclusively by its developer.

2. Authentication

The App uses OAuth 2.0 tokens provided by TikTok to authenticate API requests. These tokens are stored locally on the developer's device and are never transmitted to third parties.

3. Third-party services

The App interacts with the TikTok API solely to publish video content. No user data is shared with or collected from TikTok beyond what is required for authentication and content publishing.

4. Data storage

All data (authentication tokens, video files, posting logs) is stored locally on the developer's device. No cloud storage or external databases are used.

5. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from minors.

6. Changes

This policy may be updated at any time. Changes take effect immediately upon publication.
